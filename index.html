Today, One Step (오늘의 한 걸음)

A daily saju (Korean Four Pillars) companion. You enter your birth date once, and a friendly character named Mora gives you today's reading in a few plain sentences and one small thing to do. It is entertainment, not advice.

  • Calculation engine covering solar dates from 1908 onward in the Asia/Seoul time zone, checked against public astronomical data.
  • A versioned, human-written reading library: the same day and question always give the same answer.
  • Runs in the browser; birth details are not stored or sent to a server.

Companion

Turn a 2D character design into a 3D character that can dance, show emotion and speak naturally, and keep it as a desktop companion that remembers you.

  • Character pipeline from multi-view 2D sheets to a rigged 3D body, verified in both Blender and Unity.
  • Facial expression and speech work in progress.
  • Privacy first: conversation and memory are designed to stay on the user's own computer.
